GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions.

How much does a rooftop solar system cost?

Pricing depends on system capacity, roof conditions, equipment selection, installation requirements and applicable incentives. Contact us with your electricity bill and location for a discussion tailored to your property.

Can I get a government subsidy?

Subsidy availability depends on the scheme, system type, eligibility and current government rules. We can help you discuss the process, but approval and subsidy amounts are determined by the relevant authorities.

Which solar system is right for me?

On-grid, off-grid and hybrid systems suit different needs. Grid reliability, backup requirements, electricity usage and budget are important factors. Select “Not sure — need guidance” in the form and we can discuss your requirements.

How do I request a quotation?

Complete the consultation form above. It opens WhatsApp with your enquiry pre-filled so you can review and send it to our team.
